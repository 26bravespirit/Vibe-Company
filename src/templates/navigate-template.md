## Structural Tension Note

A-group directions tend toward **simplification, focus, and depth** — they ask "what's essential?"
B-group directions tend toward **expansion, novelty, and breadth** — they ask "what's possible?"

This tension is intentional. You're not just choosing between 10 ideas — you're choosing between two thinking modes. Both are valuable; the question is which one your project needs right now.
